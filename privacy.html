Debt Slayer privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Debt Slayer is a personal money planner made for and used by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and there are no other users, accounts or customers.

Where data is kept

  • Everything entered in the app is kept on the owner's own devices, in the browser's storage.
  • Bank balances, bank transactions and receipts are kept by the owner's own sync service, which runs in the owner's Cloudflare account. No one else runs, reads or hosts it.
  • Cloud backups are encrypted on the device with a passphrase only the owner knows, before they are stored.

Gmail (Google user data)

  • With the owner's consent, Debt Slayer asks Google for read-only access to the owner's own Gmail accounts (gmail.readonly). It never sends, changes, labels or deletes email.
  • It searches for invoices and payment receipts only. From those emails it keeps the invoice PDF or a copy of the receipt email, plus the sender, subject, date, amount, invoice number and VAT. Nothing else from the inbox is stored.
  • The data is used for one purpose: matching receipts to the owner's bank payments and exporting them for the owner's own tax return.
  • When the total of an invoice is only inside a PDF, that PDF is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to read the amount, date and invoice number. This is only to provide the feature above. The data is not used to train AI models, is not sold, and is not used for advertising.
  • Google user data is not shared with anyone else, and no person reads it except the owner.
  • The access token is stored encrypted. Access can be ended at any time in the app (Disconnect) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting revokes the token at Google.

Debt Slayer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect Gmail, hide receipts, or delete the whole sync service and its database at any time.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through the project's repository, github.com/amirsetu/debt-slayer.
